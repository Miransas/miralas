## Status

Text-to-speech works in our internal builds for English, Turkish and Russian. Public access opens with the console.

## Planned

- Type or paste text, generate audio and play it in your browser
- Uzbek voice

## Overview

Text-to-speech converts written text into natural-sounding audio. It is available today in the console.

## How to use it

1. Sign in to the console.
2. Open the **Generate** page.
3. Type or paste your text.
4. Generate and play the audio in your browser.

## Languages

English, Turkish and Russian are supported today. Uzbek is coming soon.

## Limits

Generation is rate limited per account to 10 requests per minute.
