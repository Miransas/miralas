## Overview

Miralas is a voice platform for voice cloning, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech, built with Uzbek as a first-class language.

## Available today

The console is in beta. You can:

- Sign in with your email or Google account
- Generate speech from text on the Generate page and play it in your browser (English, Turkish and Russian)

## Coming soon

- Uzbek voice
- Voice cloning profiles
- Speech-to-speech
- Developer API and SDKs
- Voice agents

Documentation for each of these will be published here as it ships. Follow the [changelog](/resources/changelog) for updates.
