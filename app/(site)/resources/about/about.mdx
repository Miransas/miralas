## About

Miralas is a voice platform for voice cloning, text-to-speech and
speech-to-speech, built with Uzbek as a first-class language. It is built by
Miransas for creators: streamers, video editors, students and anyone who needs
a voice that sounds natural in their own language.

### What we stand for

- **Consent first.** Voices are created only with the written consent of the
  voice owner, who is paid directly.
- **Quality and security come first.** We would rather ship later than ship
  something we are not proud of.

## Our story

Miransas started as a one-person studio, building and selling software
products independently. Miralas grew out of a simple problem: creators in
Uzbek-speaking communities wanted a voice that speaks their language well, and
the tools available did not give them one.

So we decided to build it ourselves. We work with real voices, recorded with
consent and paid for directly, and we are training the Uzbek model ourselves
instead of adapting it as an afterthought.

Today a small team is working on it, and Miralas is in beta. The story is
still being written; follow the [changelog](/resources/changelog) to see what
ships next.
