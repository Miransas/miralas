# Changelog

Everything that ships, changes or goes away at Miralas. Newest first.

---

## v0.4.0 — October 17, 2026
**Docs, changelog and cleaner traffic tracking**

### Added
- Documentation now lives at `/docs` with its own layout and sidebar, separate from the marketing site.
- This changelog, under Resources.
- A redirect from the old `/resources/docs` address to `/docs`, so existing links keep working.

### Changed
- The site is split into two layouts: marketing pages keep the header and footer, docs get a focused reading layout.
- Traffic attribution was rebuilt. Visits from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and other in-app browsers, UTM links and ad click IDs are now credited to the real source, and moving between our own pages no longer overwrites where a visitor came from.
- Page-view tracking counts each view exactly once, even if the browser sends it twice.
- Database connections are created on demand, which makes builds and previews more reliable.

### Fixed
- Visits from social links showing up as `miralas.io` instead of their real source.
- Inflated page-view counts caused by duplicate events.
- Missing-dependency build failures on deploy.

### Removed
- **Donation / superchat webhook.** Miralas will not read donations aloud. We are focusing on voice cloning, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech.

## v0.3.0 — October 10, 2026
**Console beta: first audio out**

### Added
- Sign in with Google.
- Text-to-speech in the console: type text on the Generate page and play the result in your browser (English, Turkish and Russian).
- Per-account rate limiting on generation (10 requests per minute).
- Dashboard pages: Home, Voices, Generate and Settings.

### Changed
- Sessions now use secure, HTTP-only cookies.
- New sign-in screen: lighter, with a static visual instead of a WebGL shader, so it runs smoothly on phones.
- Turkish and Russian pronunciation is much clearer after switching to a newer multilingual model.
