# Changelog

Website changes and engineering updates. Historical entries below describe work at the time, not a guarantee of current public access.

---

## November 18, 2026 — Product status clarification

- The active engine is Chatterbox Multilingual with the approved `t3_mtl23ls_v2.safetensors` checkpoint and 23 model-supported languages.
- **Uzbek Speech — In Development.** We're working toward native Uzbek speech capabilities through research, data collection and model evaluation. Production Uzbek inference is not available.
- The engineering baseline validates 24 kHz mono PCM16 output and a Rust API connected to Python inference over gRPC.
- Voice Catalog infrastructure is implemented. Published curated voice seeds have not been verified.
- Docker and CUDA benchmark preparation is complete; real NVIDIA GPU measurements remain pending.
- Public Console/API access and pricing are not confirmed. Speech-to-speech and voice agents remain in development.
- Qwen, CosyVoice and Miransas-native models are future exploration plans, not shipped integrations.

### Reading earlier entries

Previous references to Uzbek training, voice recordings, quotas and Console features are historical development notes. They do not establish public availability or current performance. The status above takes precedence.

## v0.4.0 — October 17, 2026
**Docs, changelog and cleaner traffic tracking**

### Added
- Documentation now lives at `/docs` with its own layout and sidebar, separate from the marketing site.
- This changelog, under Resources.
- A redirect from the old `/resources/docs` address to `/docs`, so existing links keep working.

### Changed
- The site is split into two layouts: marketing pages keep the header and footer, docs get a focused reading layout.
- Traffic attribution was rebuilt. Visits from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and other in-app browsers, UTM links and ad click IDs are now credited to the real source, and moving between our own pages no longer overwrites where a visitor came from.
- Page-view tracking counts each view exactly once, even if the browser sends it twice.
- Database connections are created on demand, which makes builds and previews more reliable.

### Fixed
- Visits from social links showing up as `miralas.io` instead of their real source.
- Inflated page-view counts caused by duplicate events.
- Missing-dependency build failures on deploy.

### Removed
- **Donation / superchat webhook.** Miralas will not read donations aloud. We are focusing on voice cloning, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech.

## v0.3.0 — October 10, 2026
## In progress

- **Console.** Accounts with email and Google sign-in, and text-to-speech in English, Turkish and Russian, work in internal builds. Public access opens when the console launches.
- **Uzbek speech research.** Research, data collection and model evaluation; no production inference.
- **Voice agent.** Early development.
- **Speech-to-speech.**
- **Developer API** with per-token limits.

### Added
- Sign in with Google.
- Text-to-speech in the console: type text on the Generate page and play the result in your browser (English, Turkish and Russian).
- Per-account rate limiting on generation (10 requests per minute).
- Dashboard pages: Home, Voices, Generate and Settings.

### Changed
- Sessions now use secure, HTTP-only cookies.
- New sign-in screen: lighter, with a static visual instead of a WebGL shader, so it runs smoothly on phones.
- Turkish and Russian pronunciation is much clearer after switching to a newer multilingual model.
