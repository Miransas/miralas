# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and maintainers, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior
* Be respectful, empathetic, and open-minded.
* Keep discussions constructive and focused on project goals.
* Accept feedback gracefully and collaborate professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior
* Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit consent.
* Any form of disruptive or unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement & Contact
Maintainers reserve the right to remove content or restrict access for behavior that violates these standards. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the repository maintainers. contact@miransas.com
